# The auxiliary function of Brownawell–Waldschmidt, and the polynomials it yields

- **Node:** `FourExp.construction_core`
- **Status:** Open
- **Theorem id:** `671c8b91-074e-431a-b4a7-fd3adb8bc8ce`
- **Source:** M. Waldschmidt, Solution du huitième problème de Schneider, J. Number Theory 5 (1973), 191–202, §III, formula (4) and Lemmas 4, 5 and 7.

**The analytic-arithmetic core of the proof.**

Let $x_1, x_2$ be $\mathbb{Q}$-linearly independent, and likewise $y_1, y_2$. Suppose every $e^{x_iy_j}$ is algebraic and $\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb{Q}}\mathbb{Q}[x_1,x_2,y_1,y_2] \le 1$. Then there are a transcendental $\omega$ and a constant $k > 0$ with the following property. For every $C$ and every large $N$, with $S, T, R_1, R_2, S'$ as in `FourExp.construction_count`, there are coefficients $c(i,j,k')$, not all zero, defining
$$G(z) = \sum_{i < S}\ \sum_{j,k' < T} c(i,j,k')\, z^i\, e^{(jx_1 + k'x_2)z},$$
such that any non-zero value $G^{(s)}(ay_1 + by_2)$ with $a < R_1$, $b < R_2$, $s < S'$ yields a non-zero $P \in \mathbb{Z}[X]$ with
$$|\text{coefficients}| \le e^{\sigma_1(N)}, \qquad \deg P \le \sigma_2(N), \qquad |P(\omega)| < e^{-C\sigma_1(N)\sigma_2(N)},$$
where $\sigma_1, \sigma_2$ are the functions of `FourExp.construction_growth` for this $k$.

**Proof** (Waldschmidt 1973, §III).
- **Setup.** Write the field as $K = L(\omega, \omega_1)$, where $L$ is the number field generated by the exponentials, $\omega$ is transcendental and $\omega_1$ is integral over $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ of degree $d$.
- **Lemma 4.** Siegel's lemma gives algebraic integers $q(\lambda)$ of size $\le \exp(k_0 t_0^2 \sqrt{\log t_0})$, not all zero. With $c(i,j,k') = \sum_{\lambda_3 < p,\,\lambda_4 < d} q(i,j,k',\lambda_3,\lambda_4)\,\omega^{\lambda_3}\omega_1^{\lambda_4}$, the function $G$ vanishes to order $s_0$ at $ay_1 + by_2$ for $a < t_1$, $b < t_2$. The $c$ are not all zero because $\omega^{\lambda_3}\omega_1^{\lambda_4}$ are independent over $L$.
- **Lemma 5.** Dividing by the zeros and applying the maximum principle gives $|G^{(s)}(t)| < \exp(-\tfrac12 t_0^4\sqrt{\log t_0})$ for $|t| \le t_0\log t_0$ and $s < \lfloor s_0/2\rfloor$. That region contains the points $ay_1 + by_2$ with $a < 14t_1$, $b < 14t_2$ once $t_0$ is large.
- **Lemma 7.** A non-zero $G^{(s_1)}(a_1y_1 + b_1y_2)$, scaled by a denominator, lies in $I_L[\omega, \omega_1]$. Its norm down to $\mathbb{Q}(\omega)$ is an integer polynomial $P$ with $\deg P \le k_7t_0^2/\sqrt{\log t_0}$, $\log H(P) \le k_6t_0^2\sqrt{\log t_0}$ and $|P(\omega)| \le \exp(-\tfrac14 t_0^4\sqrt{\log t_0})$. Since $\sigma_1(N)\sigma_2(N) = k^2N^4$, this beats every $C$ for large $N$.

**What it is for.** It is the main child of `FourExp.auxiliary_construction`, and it holds everything that uses the arithmetic of $K$.
