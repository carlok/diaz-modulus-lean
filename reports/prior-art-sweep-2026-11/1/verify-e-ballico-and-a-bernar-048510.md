# Entry 1, verification: E. Ballico and A. Bernar

- **Source read:** E. Ballico, A. Bernardi, On the X-rank with respect to linear projections of projective varieties, arXiv:0912.4834v2 (1 Jul 2010), full PDF, Remark 1, Section 2: https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.4834. Journal version (Math. Nachr.) not read.
- **Accessible:** True
- **Statement present:** True
- **Relation:** analogue only
- **Covers the part not found:** False
- **Passage:** "integral and non-degenerate subcurve of Pn of degree deg(X) = n + 1"

**Hypotheses.** Field: K algebraically closed of characteristic 0, so K = Q̄ is compatible. The source works with curves over K; it has no numbers in ℂ and no ratio h. Dimension: general n, with n = 2 giving the twisted cubic projected to a plane; n is not explicitly bounded in the remark. Conjugation: absent, with no uū, no ρ and no conic xy = ρz². Genericity: any centre O off C, and the case O on C is excluded. That excluded case is the one where the target's Möbius conclusion lives. Products, 2 × 3 configurations, progressions and H₀ never appear.

**Explanation.** Remark 1 is there. It says, without proof, that projecting a rational normal curve from a point off it gives an integral non-degenerate curve of degree n+1, with the projection as normalization. The paper is about X-rank.

My own translation shows the link. Write 1, u, ρ/u as cubics p/p, q/p, r/p in h; they span a hyperplane of cubics, which is a projection of the twisted cubic. The image lies on the conic qr = ρp². Remark 1 then rules out a centre off C, so p, q, r share a root and u is Möbius in h.

So the remark can supply the classical input for the degree step, though unique factorisation does the same directly. The translation, the conic, the excluded case O on C, the Möbius conclusion and the classification of z are not in the source. It does not state or imply the target without that added argument, and nothing in it concerns configurations. It is a background fact, not prior art.
