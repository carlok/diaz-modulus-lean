- **Passage:** "Any six points in P3, no four coplanar, lie on a unique twisted cubic"

**Hypotheses.** No match on any count. Field: C only, with no algebraically closed subfield such as Q̄ and no subspace of C over it. Dimension: P3 curves and camera projections, not 4-dimensional subspaces of a field closed under products. Conjugation: none, no uū or ū. Genericity: general cameras and curves, unrelated to u ∉ Q̄. The section's standard rational normal curve shares only the shape (s³ : s²t : st² : t³) with a progression.

**Explanation.** The paper and Section 2.2 exist as cited, though the arXiv title is Conic Multiview Varieties. Section 2.2 is computer-vision algebraic geometry: it covers projecting twisted cubics to singular plane cubics by a pinhole camera, a cusp criterion, the Chow form of the standard cubic, and two rational parametrisations from P12. It says nothing about products of linearly independent families, 2×3 configurations, four-term progressions in a subfield of C, u with uū algebraic, or Möbius functions of a ratio. A text search of the whole paper for progression, Möbius, conjugate, logarithm, Vosper, Bachoc, Diaz and Fischler found none used in this sense; "linearly independent" appears once, about Quan's constraints. The only link is that (s³ : s²t : st² : t³) resembles (b, bh, bh², bh³), a resemblance that gives no classification. The lead looks like a keyword match on "twisted cubic" and does not cover the converse classification or the degree count. Formulas were read from the text layer, not checked against rendered pages, but the verdict does not depend on them.
