**Hypotheses.** Field: source has K algebraically closed, characteristic 0; Q̄ fits. Dimension: source covers all d, and d=3 gives dim V=4, where only ℓ=1 occurs. d=3 is not excluded, and Theorem 1.1(3) gives W₁ as a conic. Conjugation: absent from the source and not needed, since ū enters only through ū=ρ/u, which gives the quadric z₁z₂−ρz₀². Genericity: none needed. h∉Q̄ makes h transcendental, so the numeric relation becomes a polynomial identity. 1, u, ρ/u are independent because u is transcendental, so the quadric has rank exactly 3. Differences: the source never mentions u, H₀ or configurations. Reaching the target also needs [BSZ17, Lemma 4] and a short translation that I did myself; it is not printed. The surjectivity in (1.2) is attributed to Park, TAMS 377 (2024), Theorem 1.2 and Prop. 3.1 (arXiv:2208.12481v2), which I opened; X must be locally factorial, which P¹ is. Only the Möbius step and the three shapes follow. The 'if' direction is not covered.

**Explanation.** All three locations exist as cited. Theorem 1.1(1) and (1.2) give Φ₃(C)=∪Wℓ(C), and Definition 3.1 gives Qℓ.

Bridge, checked by hand. Write 1, u, ρ/u as b·p₀, b·p₁, b·p₂ with p_i binary cubics in h. Then p₁p₂=ρp₀², so z₁z₂−ρz₀² is a rank-3 quadric in I(C).

By (1.2) with d=3, the quadric equals φ(f²ℓ)φ(g²ℓ)−φ(fgℓ)² with f, g, ℓ linear. Hence every p_i has the factor ℓ, and p₁=cf′²ℓ, p₂=c′g′²ℓ, p₀=c″f′g′ℓ. So u is proportional to f′/g′, a Möbius function of h.

The position of ℓ relative to f′, g′ gives the three shapes: u², ū² (1/u²), or 1/(u−a), a≠0.

This reaches the part marked not found, but as a translation, not a printed statement. The paper's own contribution (minimality) is irrelevant; the input is Park's decomposition. The fact is close to classical: rank-3 quadrics through a twisted cubic are cones with vertex on the curve. That undercuts 'not routine'. I found no dependence on the excluded repository.
