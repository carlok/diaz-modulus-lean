**Hypotheses.** Field: paper uses algebraically closed K, char 0; target uses Q̄ inside ℂ with complex conjugation. Taking K=Q̄ works only when the space lies in Q̄(u), u transcendental; the first target theorem has arbitrary g ∈ ℂ. Dimension: Lemma 5.1 (d≥5) includes d=5, a 5-dimensional section space in P^4, so it fits. Conjugation: absent; a text search finds no conjugation, pencil, invisibility or progression, and "real" appears only in a pointer to Blekherman et al. Genericity: rank of p is a condition on the centre, unrelated to the target's freeness and modulus hypotheses.

**Explanation.** The cited statements are all there: p. 2 (isomorphic projection iff rank ≥ 3), p. 9 (scroll, H+2F, from the apolar ideal), Lemma 5.1. They do not give the target.

What I can derive, for the second theorem's W only: over Q̄(u), W is a 5-dimensional base-point-free subspace of L(D), deg D = 5 (poles 0, ∞ double, −s simple), so a projection of the quintic. Theorem 3.1 with d1+d2=7 places it on S(1,2) or the cone S(0,3). A 2-minor matrix gives products in W: for S(1,2), (α,β)×(g2, g1α, g1β). So "carries a 2×3 configuration" follows, though direct computation is easier.

Not obtained: conjugation stability (I checked it by hand: the conjugate of f/(s+u) is f/s − f/(s+u); the paper has no such involution); the classification of conjugation-stable spaces as real pencil spaces, whose g need not lie in Q̄(u); and invisibility, since the paper never considers H₀+Q̄w subspaces. The part not found is therefore neither printed nor derivable from this source.
