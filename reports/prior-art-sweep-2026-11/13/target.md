# Entry 13: Polynomial hulls, and Roy's theorem with a point of a circle adjoined

Chapter 14 class: routine.

## The part not found, on its own

(a) The hull H₀ + Q̄P(u) + Q̄P(u)‾ carries a configuration exactly when deg P ∈ {2, 3}; the target is the converse for deg P ≥ 4, by a valuation count at 0 and ∞ together. (b) Real polynomials in Re u and Im u carry none. (c) Roy's strong six exponentials statement stays true when a point of a circle transcendental over the field of ℒ̃ is adjoined.

## Printed results named as nearby (these do not count as finds)

[Dia07, Théorème 7(1)] (the configurations); [BSZ17, §7] (the valuation count as a method).

## Notation

- Q̄ (`\Qbar`) is the field of algebraic numbers. ℒ is the set of logarithms of non-zero algebraic numbers; ℒ̃ = Q̄ + Q̄ℒ.
- A *point of a circle* is u ∈ ℂ, u ≠ 0, with uū algebraic. Then ū = ρ/u with ρ = uū, so conjugation acts on rational functions of u.
- H₀ = Q̄ + Q̄u + Q̄ū.
- A *p × q configuration over K* in a K-space V ⊆ ℂ is x₁,…,x_p and y₁,…,y_q, each family K-linearly independent, with every product x_i y_j in V. So a 2 × 3 configuration says V contains a product of a 2-dimensional and a 3-dimensional subspace, in the sense of all pairwise products lying in V.
- A *candidate* is u ≠ 0 with |u| and e^u algebraic; Diaz's conjecture says there is none. The *conjugation hull* of z is W_z = H₀ + Q̄z + Q̄z̄.
- A space is *invisible to four-dimensional extensions* when it carries a 2 × 3 configuration but no H₀ + Q̄w inside it carries one.

## The entry, as in chapter 14

Theorem~\ref{thm:polynomial_hull_two_by_three_configuration_iff}: a polynomial hull $\Qbar+\Qbar u+\Qbar\bar u+\Qbar P(u)+\Qbar\overline{P(u)}$ carries a configuration exactly when $\deg P\in\{2,3\}$; the configurations are \cite[Théorème 7(1)]{Dia07}, the case $P=X^{k}$ is Theorem~\ref{thm:power_hull_strong_six_exp_configuration_iff}, and the converse for $\deg P\geq4$ applies the valuation count of \cite[\S7]{BSZ17} at $0$ and $\infty$ together. Corollary~\ref{cor:real_polynomial_hull_no_two_by_three_configuration}: real polynomials in $\operatorname{Re}u$ and $\operatorname{Im}u$ carry none. Theorem~\ref{thm:logAlgTilde_adjoin_circle_point_no_two_by_three_configuration}, with Proposition~\ref{prop:rank_one_config_inverse_adjoin}: Roy's theorem stays true when a point of a circle transcendental over the field of $\widetilde{\mathcal L}$ is adjoined.

## Statements, copied from the blueprint

### DiazModulus.polynomial_hull_two_by_three_configuration_iff

Let $u\in\C\setminus\Qbar$ with $\rho=u\bar u\in\Qbar$. For $P\in\Qbar[X]$, $W_{P}=\Qbar+\Qbar u+\Qbar\bar u+\Qbar P(u)+\Qbar\overline{P(u)}$ carries a $2\times3$ configuration if and only if $\deg P\in\{2,3\}$. So at a candidate Roy's theorem excludes $P(u)$ from $\widetilde{\mathcal L}$ through its hull exactly in degrees $2$ and $3$.

Source line: The configurations of degrees $2$ and $3$ are \cite[Théorème 7(1)]{Dia07} at $v=u$, and already \cite[Théorème 2]{Dia04}; the converse was not found in the sources read; routine (Chapter~\ref{chap:14-not-found-in-the-sources}).

### DiazModulus.power_hull_strong_six_exp_configuration_iff

Let $u\in\C\setminus\Qbar$ and $k\ge1$. There are $x_1,x_2$, linearly independent over $\Qbar$, and $y_1,y_2,y_3$, linearly independent over $\Qbar$, with all six products $x_iy_j$ in $\Qbar u^{-k}+\Qbar u^{-1}+\Qbar+\Qbar u+\Qbar u^{k}$ if and only if $k=2$ or $k=3$.

Source line: Not found in the sources read; routine (Chapter~\ref{chap:14-not-found-in-the-sources}). For $k=2,3$ the configurations are geometric progressions of four elements, which \cite[Théorème 7(2)]{Dia07} excludes from $\widetilde{\mathcal L}$ with Roy's theorem (\cite[Lemma 6.1]{Fis01} excludes them from $\mathcal L$ with the six exponentials theorem); at a candidate they give \cite[Corollaire 5(1) and 5(2)]{Dia07}. The direction ``only for $k=2,3$'' was not found; Diaz remarks that inside that theorem one cannot hope to go very far \cite[p.~390]{Dia07}. It is the case $S=\{0,\pm1,\pm k\}$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:laurent_hull_config_iff}; it concerns a single power $u^{k}$ through its hull, and Proposition~\ref{prop:power_pair_hull_two_by_three_iff} still excludes pairs of higher powers.

### DiazModulus.real_polynomial_hull_no_two_by_three_configuration

Let $u\in\C\setminus\Qbar$ with $\rho=u\bar u\in\Qbar$. If $G\in\R[a,b]$ has algebraic coefficients and $z=G(\operatorname{Re}u,\operatorname{Im}u)$, then $\Qbar+\Qbar u+\Qbar\bar u+\Qbar z+\Qbar\bar z$ carries no $2\times3$ configuration.

Source line: Not found in the sources read; routine (Chapter~\ref{chap:14-not-found-in-the-sources}).

### DiazModulus.logAlgTilde_adjoin_circle_point_no_two_by_three_configuration

Assume Roy's theorem: there is no $2\times3$ configuration in $\widetilde{\mathcal L}$. If $u\bar u\in\Qbar$ and $u$ is transcendental over the field generated by $\widetilde{\mathcal L}$, there is none in $\widetilde{\mathcal L}+\Qbar u+\Qbar\bar u$ either.

Source line: Not found in the sources read; routine (Chapter~\ref{chap:14-not-found-in-the-sources}).

### DiazModulus.rank_one_config_inverse_adjoin

Let $K\subseteq F$ be subfields of $\C$, $V_{0}\subseteq F$ a $K$-space and $u$ transcendental over $F$. Every $2\times3$ configuration over $K$ in $V_{0}+Ku+Ku^{-1}$ has its products in $V_{0}$.

Source line: Not found in the sources read in this form; \cite[Proposition 12.2]{Wal00} treats $V_{0}=K$ without $u^{-1}$, and the factorisation is that of Theorem~\ref{thm:generic_circle_point_no_two_by_three_configuration}; routine (Chapter~\ref{chap:14-not-found-in-the-sources}).
